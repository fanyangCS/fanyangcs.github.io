My name is Fan Yang (杨凡 in Chinese). I am a systems researcher at [Microsoft Research Asia](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/) (MSR-Asia), where I lead [Systems Research Group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/systems-research-group-asia/) (SRG).

My research focuses on intelligent computer systems. In particular, I am interested in uncovering the fundamental principles that guide the design of [systems in the age of artificial intelligence (AI)](https://fanyangcs.github.io/projects/). 
My work has helped establish several ideas now widely used in AI systems, including hardware-aligned _tile abstractions_ for [AI compilers](https://fanyangcs.github.io/projects/aicompiler/) and _relaxed monotonicity_ for [vector stores](https://fanyangcs.github.io/projects/vectorstore/). I am also exploring how to build a general-purpose reasoning engine for complex tasks in mathematics, systems engineering, and other domains by combining [agentic](https://fanyangcs.github.io/projects/AgenticAI/) and [neuro-symbolic](https://fanyangcs.github.io/projects/neuralsymbolic/) approaches.

I also contribute to the broader research community, including through service on the program committees of ASPLOS ([2022](https://www.asplos-conference.org/asplos2022/index.html%3Fp=44.html)); EuroSys ([2023](https://2023.eurosys.org/pc.html), [2025](https://2025.eurosys.org/pc.html), [2026](https://2026.eurosys.org/pc.html)); OSDI ([2026](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/call-for-papers)); SOSP ([2026](https://sigops.org/s/conferences/sosp/2026/organizers.html)).
