The Systems and Networking Group at Microsoft Research Asia is hiring researchers in systems, networking, computer architecture, and related fields, with opportunities in Beijing, Shanghai, Hong Kong, and Singapore.

Our mission is to advance “[System Intelligence](https://www.sigops.org/2025/the-next-horizon-of-system-intelligence/)”: leveraging AI to tackle fundamental systems challenges and driving systems innovation to push the frontiers of AI.

We are exploring a new research paradigm in which AI and systems advance one another. Building on advances in AI, we rethink how complex systems are designed and built, seeking new ways to achieve high performance, correctness, and maintainability while making key system properties verifiable. These advances, in turn, inform our work on next-generation AI hardware and software. Through AI–systems co-design, we aim to overcome the limitations of today’s architectures and models.

Our ambition goes beyond making existing systems faster: we aim to transform how they are designed, built, and verified. Beyond making today’s AI better, we work alongside AI researchers to explore what comes next.

We collaborate with researchers and engineers across academia and industry in Asia, Europe, and the Americas.

Join us to tackle fundamental questions, turn bold ideas into real-world systems, and take research from ideas to deployment on AI infrastructure around the world.
